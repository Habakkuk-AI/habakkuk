# Weekly review

A weekly review answers three questions: what got done, what is stuck, and what matters next week. Fifteen minutes of the user's attention at most.

## Look back

Gather from what you can read: finished tasks and board cards, the calendar, sent email, your own sessions with the user, and last week's review if there was one.

- **Done**: what was finished. Group small things together.
- **Not done**: what was planned and did not happen, with the reason when you know it.
- **Stuck**: anything waiting on someone else, and for how long.
- **Carried over**: items that appeared in the last review and are still open. Say how many weeks.

## Look ahead

- The dates that are fixed next week: meetings, deadlines, travel, bills.
- The three things that would make next week a good one. Suggest them from what you saw, and let the user change them.
- Anything to cancel, hand to someone else or say no to. An item carried over three weeks belongs here.

## Write

One screen, in this order: a one-line verdict on the week, Done, Stuck, Next week's three, Dates. Plain statements. No praise, no pep talk.

Ask the user two questions at most, and only ones whose answers change the plan.

## After

Put the three priorities and any new tasks where the user keeps tasks. Save what they decided, so next week's review can check it. Offer to run the review at the same time every week if you can create a schedule.
