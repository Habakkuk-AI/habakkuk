# Social post

## Know before writing

1. **Where**: each network has its own length, tone and format. Ask if it was not said.
2. **Who it is for**, and what you want them to do: reply, click, share, buy, turn up.
3. **The one thing to say.** A post with two points makes neither.
4. **The voice**: read the user's recent posts if you can, and match how they write.

## Write

- **First line**: it has to earn the second. Lead with the result, the surprise, the question or the number. Never "Excited to announce".
- **Body**: one idea, in short lines. A concrete detail beats a general claim: the number, the name, the before and after.
- **Ending**: one clear thing to do, or a question people can answer in a few words.
- Write for the small screen. Blank lines between thoughts.

Fit it to the place:

- A professional network: a short story or lesson with a point. Three to eight short lines.
- A short-message network: one thought. Under the limit with room to spare. A thread only when each post stands alone.
- A photo or video network: the picture does the work. The caption adds what it cannot show.
- A local or community group: plain and useful. Who, what, where, when.

## Leave out

- Hashtags beyond two or three that people really follow.
- Emoji as decoration. One or two if the user uses them.
- Hype: "game-changing", "thrilled", "humbled".
- Claims you cannot support, other people's photos or words without credit, and anything about a named person that they would not want public.

## Hand over

Give two or three versions that differ in angle, not only in wording, and say which you would post. Note the length of each against the network's limit. Suggest what picture would go with it.

Post only when the user tells you to and has connected the account. Otherwise hand over the text.
